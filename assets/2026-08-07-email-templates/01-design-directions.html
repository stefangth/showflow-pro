How far do we push the landing-page look into email?

The "What it does" cards lean on dark gradients, glass blur, and Geist. Email clients treat those very differently from a browser. Same offer email, three honest translations. Click the one that feels right.

What actually survives an email client (Outlook Windows is the hard floor)
✓ Solid colour blocks & bulletproof buttons — everywhere △ CSS gradients — need a solid fallback (Outlook shows flat) △ Geist / web fonts — only Apple Mail & iOS; else system fallback △ Dark backgrounds — clients auto-invert unpredictably ✗ Glass / backdrop-blur — unsupported, silently drops ✗ Flexbox / grid layout — use tables

C · Dark card

The landing card itself, as an email: dark ground, glassy-looking tiles (faked with solid fills — real blur can't render), violet CTA. Boldest brand match. The honest cost: Outlook shows a flat dark box with no gradient/rounding; Gmail & Outlook.com may auto-invert the dark to grey; image-blocking and dark-on-dark hurt legibility. Highest risk, least predictable across clients.
